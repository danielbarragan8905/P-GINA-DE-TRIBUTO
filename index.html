Marie Curie

A Legacy of Scientific Discovery

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist whose research transformed our understanding of radioactivity. She became the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and remains one of the most influential scientists in history.

Together with Pierre Curie, she investigated radioactive elements and helped discover polonium and radium. Her dedication to science opened new possibilities in physics, chemistry, and medicine.

During World War I, Curie also helped develop mobile X-ray units that could be used to assist wounded soldiers. Her work continues to inspire scientists around the world.
